Dark Age Build Order (Target: 20 Population / Click Up)

    Villagers 1–3: Build 2 Houses, then gather Sheep under the Town Center (TC).

    Villagers 4–6: Continue gathering Sheep (total 6 on food).

    Villagers 7–9: Build a Lumber Camp and gather Wood (total 3 on wood).

        Note: Having 3 on wood allows you to afford an early Barracks and House.

    Villager 10: Lure the 1st Boar / Rhino.

    Villagers 11–13: Continue gathering Boar/Hunt under the TC.

        Note: Have one of these villagers build the 3rd House along your prospective wall line.

    Villager 14: Build a Barracks near your base.

        Force-drop wood if needed to afford 175 wood.

    Villager 14 (after building Barracks): Build a 4th House, then return to food.

    At the Barracks: Queue 2 Militia immediately when the Barracks completes.

        Note: Shift 1 villager from Wood back to Food around this time to maintain constant villager/uptime production.

    Villagers 15–19: Continue gathering Hunt / Food under the TC until hitting 20 population (19 Vills + Scout).

    Research Feudal Age (Click Up at 20 Pop).

        Militia Action: Send your 2 Militia + Starting Scout forward toward the opponent’s base. Focus on harassing berries and woodlines to create idle time.

Transition During Uptime (Going to Feudal)

    Wood Rebalance: Send 6 Villagers to Wood.

    Mill / Berries: Send 6 Villagers to build a Mill and gather Berries.

    Remaining Food Villagers: Finish existing hunt, then gather from straggler trees or build farms as wood allows.

    Wall / Prep: Send 1 Villager to build a House (part of your wall) and get ready to drop a Stable as soon as Feudal Age hits.

Feudal Age Transition

    At the Town Center: Research Loom immediately upon reaching Feudal Age (if not researched during Dark Age), then queue villagers.

    At the Stable: Build a Stable immediately with 1 villager and start producing Scouts.

    Economy: Continue building farms whenever wood permits, wall your base, and maintain double-pronged pressure with your surviving Militia and Scouts.
